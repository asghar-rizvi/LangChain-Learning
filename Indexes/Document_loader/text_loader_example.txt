The sun shines bright upon the grassy field, 
As two brave teams prepare to never yield. 
The bowler runs and flings the leather ball,
While batsmen stand up straight and ready, tall. 
A mighty swing, the willow meets the core, 
The crowd erupts and shouts for something more. 
It races to the rope for four quick runs, 
Under the heat of golden summer suns. 
A game of skill, of patience, and of heart, 
Where every single player plays their part.
